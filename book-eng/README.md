# Agent Systems (English Edition)

An introduction to designing, building, evaluating, and operating agent systems built on large language models.

Writing in this field tends to sit at two extremes. On one side are guides to a particular
framework, which go stale when the framework changes. On the other are research papers that
propose one technique and say nothing about how a whole system fits together. This book tries to
fill the space between. It writes down why a part is needed, when not to use it, and what you pay
when you pick it.

## What Is Inside

Seven parts, twenty-three chapters, 302 pages.

1. **What Is an Agent**: the whole structure and the shape of the loop. What counts as an agent, and who decides the next step
2. **Working with Context**: the unit of the budget and how assembly works. What to put into a finite context, and when
3. **Tools and the Outside World**: the way out to the environment. Tools and what they return, how capability gets supplied, what form the output takes
4. **Knowledge and Memory**: the machinery for finding what you left outside, and memory that survives a session
5. **Running the Loop**: control that owns the cycle from outside. When to stop, what to keep, how to start again
6. **Evaluation and Observability**: how you know something got better, and what a run has to leave behind to be examined later
7. **Operations, Safety, Governance**: who stands in front of a result you cannot undo, and how to take in text that comes from outside

## Who It Is For

Written for people who have called a language model API themselves at least once, and for people
who want to understand agent systems as a whole.

## Feedback

Feedback from engineers and researchers is welcome. Three things in particular.

- **Where it does not match practice.** Passages describing something no one actually does
- **Where a citation or a fact is wrong.** Details of a specification, who published it, when
- **Whether the "Try this today" items can be done today.** Whether each one is small enough to pick up right after reading
